Help with recording, iCloud sync, and your Timeline.

Keep your location private.

You do not need to send a route, a Timeline screenshot, an export, or an iCloud identifier. Your app version, device type, and a description of what happened are enough to start.

Common questions

Where is my history stored?

New observations are saved on the iPhone first, then synchronized through the private CloudKit database belonging to the Apple account signed into iCloud. There is no separate Where I Went account or developer-operated location server.

Why does an older day take a moment to appear?

If a day is not stored on this device, the app loads its points from your private iCloud. Stay online and signed into iCloud while it loads.

Will deleting the app delete my iCloud history?

No. Deleting the app does not delete the private iCloud library. Use the separate Delete All Location History command inside the app only when you intend to remove the library permanently.

Does Motion & Fitness have to be enabled?

No. It improves activity recognition and battery decisions, but location-only recording continues to work when Motion & Fitness permission is declined.

How do I pause recording?

Open Timeline and use the Pause control in the bottom panel. Existing history remains available.
