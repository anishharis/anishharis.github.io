Drinkmark

A private drink journal for iPhone.

Notice your patterns

Record drinks, review your calendar, and confirm drink-free days. Missing days stay unknown until you record them.

Your journal is stored on your iPhone. Apple Health is optional. You can also ask read-only questions about your recorded history, set weekend reminders, and review optional possible-visit suggestions. A suggestion or an Assistant answer never records a drink.

The journal works on iOS 18 or later. The on-device Assistant requires iOS 26 or later and an eligible, ready Apple Intelligence device.
