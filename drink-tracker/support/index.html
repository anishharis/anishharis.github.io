Support

Help with your drink journal and its optional features.

Support requests are public.

Do not post drink or Apple Health history, journal screenshots, exported CSV files, precise locations, account or device identifiers, phone numbers, or email addresses. Use made-up dates and counts to explain a problem.

Report a problem

Open a GitHub support request for a bug, a question, or an improvement. Include the app version if you know it, your iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. You need a GitHub account to submit a request.

Common questions

What does a question mark mean?

The day is not logged. It is different from a confirmed zero. In a day's small calendar panel, the first plus marks an unlogged day as zero; another plus adds one standard drink. To record a drink immediately for today, use Log 1 drink on Home.

How do I change an older day?

Use the calendar arrows or month/year picker to find it. Tap a date for the small count panel, or hold a date to open its full editor. Select days creates a draft of past drink-free days; only the explicit Mark button saves that draft. Cancel leaves those days unchanged.

Where is my journal stored?

The app saves your journal on your iPhone. There is no separate Drinkmark account or developer-operated journal server. Optional Apple Health access can read drink records and save drinks you log; Health does not contain every part of your journal, such as confirmed drink-free days and app settings.

Apple Health counts look incomplete. What should I check?

Open Settings in Drinkmark to review its Health status. Check the Health permissions you selected in Apple's system privacy controls. Read and write access are separate: successfully saving a drink does not establish that the app can read every Health record.

Why is the Assistant unavailable?

The history Assistant uses Apple's on-device model. It needs iOS 26 or later, an eligible Apple Intelligence iPhone, Apple Intelligence enabled, a supported language, and a ready downloaded model. The rest of the journal works without it. Answers are read-only and cannot add, remove, or change drinks.

Does a possible visit mean I drank?

No. Nearby venues can be confused, and iPhone may report a visit late or miss it. Possible visits are optional suggestions for you to review. They never add drinks automatically. You can change the feature and its location access in Settings.

Review shows the venue on Apple Maps when it can load. If Maps cannot load, you can still review the visit by date.

Can I keep a separate record?

Use Export CSV in Settings to create a separate record. The export contains private journal information. Choose where to keep or share it carefully, and never attach it to a public support request.
