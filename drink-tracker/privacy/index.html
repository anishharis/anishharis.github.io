Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

This policy covers the Drink Tracker iPhone app and this support website. The app has no account, advertising, tracking SDK or developer-operated server for personal journal data. It does not automatically send your journal, locations or Assistant questions to the developer.

Your journal and local storage

Your drink entries, drink-free confirmations, settings and import receipts are stored locally. If your journal contains previously imported history, import receipts retain the original imported dates and counts to prevent duplicate imports, even after you delete an imported entry.

iOS may include the journal and preferences in device backups, depending on your settings. The separate routine-area coordinate file uses iOS file protection and is excluded from device backups.

Optional Apple Health

With your permission, Drink Tracker reads Alcohol Consumption records and saves or deletes records created by this app. Other apps’ Health records are read-only here. Imported daily history is not uploaded to Health.

Apple manages the Health store and any Health iCloud synchronization you enable. Revoking access or deleting Drink Tracker does not remove records already saved in Health. You can manage those records and permissions in Apple Health and iPhone Settings.

Optional location features and Apple Maps

Possible visits uses visit locations for Apple Maps venue searches. The routine-area map also sends searches and map regions to Apple. A nearby match does not establish that you visited a place or drank.

Routine learning is on by default, but runs only with possible visits and location access enabled. Approximate areas and stop evidence remain on your iPhone; they are not included in Assistant context or CSV exports. You can turn learning off in Possible visits settings.

When the app runs, it prunes learning evidence after 28 days, nearby suggestions and review context after 7 days, and review decisions after 30 days. Saved routine areas, normal-suggestion choices and ignored places remain until you remove them.

On-device Assistant

Your questions and selected journal facts are processed by Apple’s on-device language model. The app does not send those prompts or answers to a cloud AI service. Assistant answers do not edit your journal.

Weekend reminders

Optional weekend notifications are scheduled locally by iOS. The app does not send your journal to a push-notification server, and reminders never log drinks automatically.

Exports and sharing

CSV exports contain journal dates, counts, record identifiers, sources and any serving details, including visible Health records. Temporary files are created locally. You choose whether and where to share them; the recipient or service handles shared files under its own policies.

The app does not automatically upload exports or support reports. Avoid including health records, location details, private screenshots or exported files in a public support request.

Your controls and deletion

Remove journal entries in day details; Health changes may wait for synchronization. In Possible visits, you can clear visit history, reset routine areas or remove saved place choices. Manage Health, location and notification access in iPhone Settings.

Deleting the app removes its local app data. It does not delete Apple Health records, files you exported or shared, or existing device backups. Those copies must be managed in the relevant Apple settings or destination service.

This website

The app does not send journal or location data to this website. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, cookies or submission forms to these pages.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub maintains ordinary web request logs and states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, even when visitors are not signed in. This hosting-related processing is separate from the app’s local data and is covered by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you choose to open a GitHub support issue, the report is public and the developer can read what you submit. Share only a description of the problem without personal journal or location data.
